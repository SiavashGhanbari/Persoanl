About

Hi there.

I'm a product designer who works on the parts of a product that make or lose money: sign-up, checkout, payments, and the small moments in between.

Most recently I worked on Zarpin, a digital gold app with 400K+ users. I moved the team's focus from visuals to flows, found where users were dropping off, and redesigned the gold purchase flow so more people actually finish it.

Before that, at Bitpin, a crypto exchange, I cut toman withdrawal support tickets by 35% by making slow bank payouts visible instead of silent.

How I work: start from the numbers, trace each drop-off to a real moment of confusion, then remove steps and build components that can be reused across every flow.
